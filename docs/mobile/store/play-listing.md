## Full description (≤4000)

Apna Invoice is GST billing made simple for Indian shops, traders, service businesses and freelancers. Make a proper GST tax invoice on your phone in under a minute, send it on WhatsApp, and always know who owes you money. The same account works on apnainvoice.com, so your CA sees exactly what you see.

GST INVOICES IN SECONDS
• Pick a customer, add items from your saved list, and issue — invoice numbers are given automatically
• CGST + SGST or IGST is worked out for you from the customer's state
• GST slabs 0%, 5%, 12%, 18%, 28% and 40%, HSN/SAC codes, units
• Transport details, e-way bill number, terms and notes
• Original, duplicate and triplicate PDF copies, exactly as on the web

GET PAID FASTER
• Share the invoice PDF or a secure link on WhatsApp in one tap
• Record payments by UPI, cash, NEFT, IMPS, RTGS, cheque or card
• Part payments and balance due are tracked automatically
• Send polite payment reminders for overdue invoices
• See outstanding and overdue amounts on your home screen

QUOTATIONS THAT TURN INTO INVOICES
• Track quotations as draft, sent, accepted or declined
• Convert an accepted quotation into an invoice in one tap

CUSTOMERS AND LEDGER
• Search customers by name, phone or GSTIN
• Customer ledger with every invoice, payment and running balance
• Call or WhatsApp a customer straight from their ledger

ONE ACCOUNT, PHONE AND WEB
• Everything syncs with apnainvoice.com
• Purchases, expenses, stock, GSTR-1, GSTR-3B, HSN summary and reports are on the web, and are coming to the app
• Team access for your accountant or billing staff, with roles

SAFE AND PRIVATE
• Your data is encrypted in transit and stored securely
• No ads and no tracking
• Delete your account any time from inside the app

Made in India for Indian businesses. Free during beta.
